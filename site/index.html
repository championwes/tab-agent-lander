FAQ

Questions agents ask on the discovery call.

What's the actual commission structure?

Domestic agents are tiered off their top-line customer revenue (not gross profit): 65% up to $5M, 70% from $5M–$10M, 75% from $10M–$20M, and 80% above $20M. International agents earn a flat 60%. Tier raises are automatic as your revenue crosses each threshold — you don't have to ask for them, and they've never changed in 22 years.

Will I lose customers when I transition?

Less than 5% of customers are lost in TAB transitions. Most agencies are happy if 70% of a "fully committed" book actually converts. The difference is our Agent Development team — we sit with you through customer onboarding, credit setup (1–2 hours per account), and your first loads, instead of handing you a portal and wishing you luck.

Why is the calculator based on revenue and not gross profit?

Because the TAB tiers are off the revenue you bill the customer — that's a meaningful advantage. Most agency programs tier off gross profit, which can be inflated, deflated, or interpreted differently. Tiering off top-line revenue is cleaner and harder to game in either direction.

What do I actually need to get started?

A computer, a phone, high-speed internet, transportation experience, and a customer book of business. Everything else — TMS, load boards, lead data, insurance, surety bond, authority, billing, claims — is on TAB.

What is the Agent Development team and why does it matter?

It's a dedicated team that advocates for you internally — fighting for credit limits with accounting, escalating customer issues, helping you navigate the system in your first weeks. Most agencies have an onboarding team and then "good luck, email us." TAB built the Agent Development team because we kept hearing from agents that real, ongoing support was the thing they were missing everywhere else.

Who do I report to day-to-day?

You run your own book. TAB is a partner and a back office, not a manager. Operational support is 24/7 when you need it.
